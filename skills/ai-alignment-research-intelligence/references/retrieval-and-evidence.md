# Retrieval and evidence protocol

Search arXiv cs.AI/cs.LG/cs.CL/cs.CV/stat.ML and relevant categories; OpenReview/proceedings; original author/lab reports including Transformer Circuits, Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, Redwood, METR, Apollo and AISI; academic groups and research discussions. Treat this as a nonexclusive list. Record blocked sources.

Combine date filters with circuit/SAE/superposition; anisotropy/participation ratio/effective rank/CKA; reward hacking/deception/scheming/generalization; activation patching/ablation/steering; monitorability/construct validity/oversight; identifiability/causal abstraction/formal alignment. Adapt vocabulary from discoveries. Add critique, null, replication, failure, confound and counterexample searches for principal claims.

Read original full text for deep reviews. Record page/section/table/figure locators. Preserve statistical units: random seeds, rollouts, prompts, classes and models are different units. Do not assume dependent class pairs or repeated prompts are independent. Check holdout leakage, selection on maxima, multiplicity, control matching and measurement validity. Distinguish percentage points from relative percent change.

Assess dimensions separately:

| Dimension | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| Rigor | Controls, uncertainty, units, robustness, leakage, selection |
| Reproducibility | Exact versions, materials, hardware, access; linked is not reproduced |
| External validity | Tested models/tasks/distributions and omitted settings |
| Causal identification | Specific intervention, artifacts, compensation, rivals |
| Independent corroboration | Independent teams and genuine replication |

Use Unclear for insufficient access, Limited for identified restrictions, Moderate for bounded support, Strong for strong support on the narrow dimension. Explain labels without averaging them.

Do not equate benchmark outcomes with deployment prevalence, probe accuracy with information use, or ablation damage with unique mechanism identity. Treat forecasts as forecasts. Preserve unresolved discrepancies. Verify dates from primary histories; do not infer them from numeric IDs. Mark same-day items with unavailable release times as cutoff-time unverified.
